# Hi, I'm Miguel

I design and review PCBs for small companies and startups. I work mostly in KiCad and EasyEDA, and every board I deliver is ready to order at JLCPCB, with Gerbers, BOM and placement files.

Before freelancing I spent years writing on-board software for European Space Agency satellites at Thales Alenia Space, and navigation systems for the Spanish Navy at GMV. That is where I learned to design for reliability, and I bring the same habits to every board, big or small.

I offer my services under the name [Copperpath PCB](https://copperpathpcb.com).

---

## What I can help you with

- **PCB Design:** from your schematic or your requirements to a KiCad or EasyEDA project ready for manufacturing
- **PCB Review:** a findings report on your schematic and layout before you spend money on boards
- **PCB Rescue:** for boards that don't work as expected, or designs made with AI tools that need a professional pass
- **Firmware and hardware testing:** as an extra, quoted separately
